# Code of conduct

KaadzOS is a small, high-signal project. We want it to stay that way.

## What we expect

- Be precise. Describe the problem, the version and what you tried.
- Be kind. Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most mistakes are not malice.
- Keep scope tight. A small patch is easier to review than a large one.
- Respect the palette: the OS is black and white; conduct is no different.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation, doxxing, or posting private security reports in public.

## Reporting

Email **kaadz@protonmail.com**. Reports are read by the project maintainer. Persistent or severe behaviour can lead to removal from project spaces.

This conduct applies to issues, pull requests and every other KaadzOS project space.
